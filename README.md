# Procedural memory migration

Prepared 17 September 2026 as an independent Construct-2 ancillary study.
**Status: bounded investigation commissioned; no experiments started by preparation.**

## Question

Can paid experience with a previously unseen recipient improve the choice to
retain, adapt or retire acquired procedural guidance enough to justify its
cost, compared with competent unchanged inheritance and reconstruction?

The broader program asks how agents accumulate useful experience across sessions
and where it should live. A lesson valuable to one model may be redundant,
misleading or awkward for another. We want to understand that change in value,
including cases where unchanged inheritance or retirement is the best choice.
The investigator owns workload discovery, methods, execution and publication.

## Starting comparison

Establish functioning procedural guidance, then hold its source bank and
underlying experience fixed while changing the receiving model. Initially keep
facts and task obligations fixed. Compare inheritance with the recipient's own
competent behavior; better absolute performance after an upgrade is insufficient
evidence that inherited guidance helped. A harness change is a possible later
extension, not a required second axis.

Useful alternatives include competent retrieval from the unchanged bank, the
recipient without inherited guidance, reconstruction from common source
experience, and a modest adaptation or retirement rule informed by paid recipient
experience. Choose the contrasts needed to identify an explanation; a large
factorial is unnecessary. Preserve ordinary tools, tests and feedback across
alternatives. A target absent from model-identity labels needs a competent
shared-bank fallback, not an artificially empty retrieval result.

Use developed guidance and serious alternatives before interpreting a negative
result. Do not hide source experience from reconstruction or equate a rewrite
with a useful adaptation. Count inspection, calibration, adaptation, validation,
failed attempts and subsequent use. Distinguish source-bank construction from
incremental migration, while accounting for both in a lifetime claim. Keep
quality, tokens, latency and compute visible rather than concealing incomplete
tasks in cost-per-success averages. Research search cost and deployment cost
are different quantities; retain both where relevant.

Evaluate developed claims on fresh complete tasks after calibration. A post-hoc
best action or accurate migration forecast is not an implemented decision rule.
A simple policy, unchanged memory or retirement can resolve the comparison.
Close a bounded phase on explanatory progress, a demonstrated limitation or a
concrete resource constraint. No positive adaptation result is required.

## What the public research already answers

- [Memory Transfer Learning, 2604.14004v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.14004v1),
  §§3.1–3.2/4.4, and [CONTRAMEM, 2608.22533v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.22533v1),
  Methodology and Appendix A.7, already support transfer comparisons. Include a
  strong unchanged bank; generic unseen-recipient transfer is not our contribution.
- [MemCollab, 2603.23234v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.23234v2), §§2.1–2.3,
  Appendix E, already uses model-aware selection. Our candidate concerns a
  recipient outside the bank's construction histories and the cost of learning
  what helps it.
- [Does Your Agent's Memory Survive a Model Upgrade?, 2609.05339v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.05339v1),
  §§2–3.2, Appendices C–E, already tests factual-store migration and cheap
  forecasting. This study concerns decisions about procedural guidance.
- [Agent KB, 2507.06229v5](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.06229v5), §§3.3/4.3,
  supplies feedback-conditioned refinement; [Agent Memory Distillation,
  2608.07169v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.07169v1), §§3.2–3.3, supplies
  hierarchical construction. These are possible donors, not mandatory methods.

## Prospective expectations — PM1–PM3

Copied from the root's [selection note](../../construct-2/notes/EXPERIENCE_PORTABILITY.md)
before execution; preserve these statements when recording subsequent assessment.

- **PM1:** The useful value of inherited procedural guidance will depend on the recipient; some guidance that helps its source may be redundant or harmful after a model change. Uniformly useful inheritance would weaken this expectation within the tested scope.
- **PM2:** Paid recipient experience will improve adaptation or retirement decisions only where it distinguishes consequential alternatives. A strong unchanged bank, simple rule or observation cost may remove its advantage.
- **PM3:** Access to the same source experience will make competent reconstruction a stronger competitor than adaptation from the inherited artifact alone. Selective adaptation can still repay its cost when useful guidance survives and reconstruction repeats substantial work.
